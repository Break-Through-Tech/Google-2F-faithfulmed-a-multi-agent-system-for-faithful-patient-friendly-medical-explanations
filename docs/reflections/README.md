# Individual Reflections

This folder contains concise, individual progress reflections. Each teammate writes their own file; reflections are not shared team-wide documents.

## Schedule

- **September 2026:** Each of the 5 team members creates exactly one monthly reflection covering what they personally worked on during September. Use `reflections/september/` and do not create weekly September files.
- **October 2026 onward:** Every team member creates one reflection every week. Organize files by month, such as `october/`, `november/`, and so on. Name each file with the consistent `YYYY-MM-DD-name.md` format, for example `2026-10-05-name.md`.

## What to include

Use `templates/weekly-reflection-template.md` for both the September monthly reflection and later weekly reflections. Cover:

- What you worked on
- What you learned
- Results or findings
- Challenges or blockers
- Decisions or contributions
- Next steps

Keep reflections concise, but specific enough to show personal contribution and progress. Be factual and describe work that was actually completed.

## Contribution rules

Create your own reflection on your branch, use a clear filename, and open a PR. Do not overwrite another person's file or put multiple people's updates into one shared file. Individual files keep ownership clear and reduce merge conflicts.
