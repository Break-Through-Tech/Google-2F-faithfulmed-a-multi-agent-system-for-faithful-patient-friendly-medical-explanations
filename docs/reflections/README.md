# Individual Reflections

This folder contains concise, individual progress reflections. Each teammate writes their own file; reflections are not shared team-wide documents.

## Schedule

- **September 2026:** Each of the 5 team members creates exactly one monthly reflection covering what they personally worked on during September. Use `reflections/september/` and do not create weekly September files.
- **October 2026 onward:** Every team member creates one reflection every two weeks, with separate Week 1 and Week 2 sections inside the same file. The first period is October 1–14 (Week 1: October 1–7; Week 2: October 8–14); the next is October 15–28. Continue in consecutive 14-day periods. Organize files by the month in which the period ends. Name each file for the period's end date using `YYYY-MM-DD-name.md`, for example `october/2026-10-14-name.md`. An in-progress report may be started before its end date; label unfinished days clearly.

## What to include

Use `templates/biweekly-reflection-template.md` for October onward. The existing September monthly reflections retain their own format. Cover these topics separately for each week:

- What you worked on
- What you learned
- Results or findings
- Challenges or blockers
- Decisions or contributions
- Next steps

Keep reflections concise, but specific enough to show personal contribution and progress. Be factual and describe work that was actually completed.

## Contribution rules

Create your own reflection on your branch, use a clear filename, and open a PR. Do not overwrite another person's file or put multiple people's updates into one shared file. Individual files keep ownership clear and reduce merge conflicts.
