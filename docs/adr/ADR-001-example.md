Proposed

## Date

YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and forces that require a decision. Keep this specific enough that someone who was not in the discussion can understand the situation.

## Decision

State the decision clearly and directly.

## Why

Explain why this option was selected and how it addresses the context.

## Alternatives Considered

List the credible alternatives and briefly explain why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Describe the expected benefits, tradeoffs, risks, and impacts on future work.

## Follow-Up

List follow-up tasks, owners if known, open questions, or a later review date.
